Your 10-minute drill

Do these in order. The point is to notice the data, not win a game.

1
Move the target

Drag the orange puck and watch target error change.

2
Teleoperate

Use shoulder and elbow to bring the gripper closer.

3
Record an episode

Capture observation–action pairs while you move.

4
Replay it

The same actions reproduce the trajectory.

5
Run the baseline

Compare your demo with a scripted inverse-kinematics policy.

Who owns what?

These projects complement each other; they are not substitutes.

MuJoCoLoads the robot and world, resolves dynamics and contacts, advances the state.
LeRobotRepresents datasets, policies, training runs, teleoperation and evaluation.
Your envTranslates MuJoCo state into LeRobot observations and actions into actuator commands.
ROS 2 laterConnects the trained behavior to real cameras, motors, safety and system telemetry.
